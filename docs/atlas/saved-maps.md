# Saved maps

**Saved maps** are custom map snapshots that store your layers and markers separately from your main map data. They are fully independent, so changes to saved maps do not affect your main layers or markers and vice versa.

You can open the **My Maps** panel from the map toolbar or by pressing the <kbd>B</kbd> key.

## Creating new maps

There are two methods of creating new maps:

1. **Creating a new map**
   - You can create a blank new map by clicking <icon name="add"></icon> **Create new map** in the **My Maps** panel.

2. **Saving a shared map**
   - You can also save shared maps by clicking <icon name="save"></icon> **Save current map** in the **My Maps** panel, while browsing a shared map.

## Working with maps

- Hovering over the <icon name="savedMaps"></icon> icon next to the map name will show a preview of the map.
- By clicking <icon name="view"></icon> **View**, a saved map will be loaded into your map in **Edit mode**, replacing the main content.
- When in **Edit mode**, you can update a saved map's layers and markers through their respective panels in the map toolbar.
