# Exporting & sharing maps

Atlaset makes it easy to export your map data, share your travels with others and embed interactive maps on your website or blog.

## Exporting map data

Exported map data can include:

- **Visited countries**
- **Layers** (all visible)
- **Markers** (all visible)
- **Map name**
- **Sharer name**

## Downloading maps

### As an image

You can download your map as an image to share or use in presentations, reports, or social media. To do that, choose your desired format, adjust the settings and click <icon name="download"></icon> **Download**.

- Supported image formats are `PNG`, `SVG`, `JPEG`, `WebP`.
- The exported image will include all visible data on the map, including the currently selected map settings.

### As a data file

Download your layers and/or markers as a JSON file for backup or sharing. To do that, select the `JSON` format, adjust your desired data and click <icon name="download"></icon> **Download**. 

- The export settings at the top affect the data that will be included in the file.

> <icon name="tip"></icon> **Tip**
>
> Export your data regularly for backup. You can import exported JSON files back into the app at any time!

## Sharing maps

Atlaset supports sharing your maps (visited countries, layers and/or markers) via a special URL:

- Use <icon name="share"></icon> **Share** to generate a special URL containing your map data.
- Share the link with friends—anyone with the link can view your map in **readonly** mode.

## Embedding maps

Atlaset supports embedding maps directly into your website or blog using an HTML `<iframe>`. This allows you to display interactive maps anywhere online.

- Use <icon name="code"></icon> **Embed** to generate an `iframe` link.
- Copy the provided HTML `<iframe>` code into and paste it into your website's HTML where you want the map to appear.
- You can see an example [here](/docs/examples/embedded-map.html).

## Privacy

- Shared maps are independent of your account and do not expose your private data.
- You control what is included in the shared map.
