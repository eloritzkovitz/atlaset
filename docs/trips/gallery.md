# Trip gallery

Trips can include a built-in photo gallery and an optional link to an external photo album.

## Adding photos to the gallery

- Open the **Photos** tab when creating or editing a trip.
- Upload your images. Supported formats are `JPEG`, `PNG` or `WebP`.
- Each trip supports up to **10** uploaded photos.

Uploaded photos are shown in the **Gallery** section of the trip details page.

## Managing photos in the gallery

- Photos can be reordered by dragging them or by using the **Move photo up** or **Move photo down** controls.
- Photos can be deleted individually by clicking **Delete photo**.

## External photo albums

The **Photo Album URL** can be optionally filled with a URL to your external album on the cloud, linking it with the trip. When a link is available, it can be opened by clicking on the <icon name="photoAlbum"></icon> **View Photo Album** button from the gallery or from trip actions menu.
