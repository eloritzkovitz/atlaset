# Trip Gallery

Trips can include a built-in photo gallery and an optional link to an external photo album.

## **Managing trip photos**

- Open the `Photos` tab when creating or editing a trip.
- Upload `JPEG`, `PNG` or `WebP` images.
- Each trip supports up to `10` uploaded photos.
- Remove individual photos from the gallery before saving the trip.

Uploaded photos are shown in the `Gallery` section of the trip details page.

## **External photo albums**

Add an optional `photo album URL` to link the trip to your external album on the cloud. When a link is available, it can be opened from the gallery or trip actions.
