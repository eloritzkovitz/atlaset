# Friends

In Atlaset, you can stay connected with other people. Whether they are family, friends or travel partners - friends can connect together, share content and collaborate.

## Adding friends

You can add friends by using the **user search**, where you can find users by name or username. Once you find the friend you want to add, visit their profile and click on the **Add Friend** button.

## Friend requests

Friend requests allow you to manage pending friendship connections.

- **Incoming requests** can be **accepted** or **rejected**.
- **Outgoing requests** can be **withdrawn** as long as the recipient has not responded.
- Once an incoming request is accepted, the two users become friends.

## The Friends panel

You can view your friends and manage friend requests from the **Friends** panel, accessible from the **user men**u or by pressing the <kbd>N</kbd> key.

The panel contains two sections:

- **Friends** — search your friends, open their profiles, and perform available friend actions.
- **Friend requests** — respond to incoming requests and view or manage outgoing requests.

## Managing friends

By clicking on a friend's profile, a profile page will open, where you can see information about the user. Friend relationships can also be managed from the available friend actions.

Friendship does not necessarily make all profile information or content visible. Visibility depends on the privacy and sharing settings of the relevant content.

## Profiles and shared content

Friends can interact between themselves based on their travels and content. For example, the **Visits** section in the user's profile allows you to compare your visited countries with your friend.

Friends can also share trips between them by **granting access permissions** or **adding people as participants**.
