# Your profile

Your profile is your personal space in Atlaset. It contains information about you, your travel activity, friends and achievements. Through your privacy settings, you can control who can view your profile and its information.

## Profile sections

Your profile is divided into several sections:

- **About**: Displays a summary of your personal information.
- **Friends**: Displays your friends. When viewing another user's profile, the user's friends and your mutual friends.
- **Visits**: Displays the countries you have visited and the countries you want to visit.

## About

The **About** section can include:

- Personal details, such as your home country, birthday and join date
- Biography
- Social media links
- Travel summary
- Best leaderboard scores, when available

Your email address is only displayed on your own profile.

### Travel summary

Your travel summary includes:

- **Visited** — countries recorded through completed trips or manually added to your visited countries.
- **Want to visit** — countries you have added to your **Want to Visit** list.

## Friends

The **Friends** section displays the user's friends and their total friend count.

When viewing another user's profile, you can also view **mutual friends** — friends you have in common with that user.

You can open a friend's profile directly from the list.

## Visits

The **Visits** section lets you browse a user's travel history through two lists:

- **Visited** — countries the user has visited.
- **Want to visit** — countries the user plans or hopes to visit.

When viewing another user's profile, you can compare your visited countries by clicking <icon name="compare"></icon> **Compare**.

The comparison shows:

- Countries both users have visited
- Countries only you have visited
- Countries only the other user has visited
- The total number of countries visited by each user
- The number of countries visited by both users

## Updating your profile

You can edit your profile by clicking <icon name="editField"></icon> **Edit Profile** on your own profile.

Depending on the available profile settings, you can update your personal information, biography, social media links and other profile details.

## Profile privacy

Profiles can be public or private.

A private profile restricts profile content from users who are not allowed to view it. Friends can still view a private profile, while users who do not have access will see that the profile is private.

Profile search visibility is also controlled separately, so a profile can be excluded from user search and indexing.
