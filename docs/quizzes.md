# Quizzes

Atlaset features interactive geography quizzes to help you learn and test your knowledge about the world. Compete for high scores, challenge your friends and climb the leaderboards!

## Quiz Types

- **Guess the Flag:** Guess countries based on the flag.
- **Guess the Capital:** Guess capital cities of countries.

## How to Play

1. **Start a Quiz:**
   - Go to the Quizzes section from the main menu or dashboard.
   - Choose your quiz type, difficulty and game mode.
2. **Answer Questions:**
   - Enter your answer in the provided form.
   - Get instant feedback and move to the next question.
3. **Game Over:**
   - See your score, correct answers and stats.
   - Check the leaderboards for the best scores!

## Leaderboards

- Compete with other users for the top scores.
- View leaderboards based on quiz type and difficulty.

## Tips

- Use keyboard shortcuts for faster answers.
- Review your mistakes after each quiz to learn more.

Enjoy challenging yourself and learning new facts with Atlaset quizzes!
