# Account & Profile

Your Atlaset account lets you save your personal data, customize your experience and connect with `friends`. The profile features help you manage your personal information and showcase your journeys.

## **Creating an Account**

- Sign up with your `email` or by using your `Google` account.
- Your account securely stores your `map data`, `trips`, `visited countries` and `settings`.

## **Account Settings**

- Change your `password` or linked authentication methods.
- Manage connected `devices` and `sessions`.
- Update `privacy settings` and `notification preferences`.

## **Profile Information**

- View and edit your `personal details`.
- View your `visited countries` in your `profile`.
- Your profile info is visible to `friends` and on `leaderboards`.

## **Friends & Social**

- Find and add `friends` to share your journeys with.
- View friends’ `profiles` and compare travel stats.

> **Tips:**
> - Keep your `profile` up to date for a personalized experience.
> - Use `privacy settings` to control what others can see.
> - Lost access? Use the `password reset` or `account recovery` options.
