# Your Profile

Your profile shows your personal information - including personal details, biography, social media links, visited countries and more. Through privacy, you can control what information people can see.

## **Profile Information**

- Your `personal details`
- Social media links
- Your `visited countries`
- Leaderboard positions (if any)

## **Updating Your Profile**

You can edit your personal information by clicking the `Edit Profile` button. `Visited countries` are based on your `trip data`, and `leaderboards` are based on your best scores, if they exist in the `leaderboards`.
