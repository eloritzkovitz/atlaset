# **Timezones**

The **`Timezones`** section gives you a detailed list of timezones, ordered by `UTC offsets`. Each timezone shows a detailed view, including countries or territories where the timezone is used, either as **standard time** or as **daylight saving time** (if applicable).

Timezones can be searched and filtered by UTC offsets.
