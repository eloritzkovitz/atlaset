# Activity

The **Activity Log** keeps a chronological record of actions and events associated with your account.

To open the activity log, you can enter the dashboard or your profile.

## Activity entries

Each entry includes:

- An icon indicating the type of activity.
- A description of the action.
- The date and time it occurred.

Activity entries are displayed with the most recent activity first. More entries are loaded automatically as you scroll.

## Managing activity

You can remove individual entries from your activity log. To do so, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the <icon name="dashboard"></icon> **Dashboard**.

2. Click **Activity** to open the activity log.

3. Find the correct activity you would like to remove and click <icon name="remove"></icon> **Delete activity**.

> <icon name="info"></icon> **Note**
>
> Deleting an activity entry removes it from your activity log but does not undo the action that originally generated it.
