# **Statistics**

The **`Statistics`** section provides insights and analytics about your travel history. Features include:

- **Trip overview**: See a summary of your `trips`, including a breakdown of your trips by type and status and other statistics.
- **Trip history**: Browse a `timeline` of your `trips`, with details on `destinations`, `dates` and `notes`.
- **Yearly and monthly breakdowns**: Analyze your travel patterns over time, with charts and tables for each `year` and `month`.
