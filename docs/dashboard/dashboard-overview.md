# Dashboard overview

The **Dashboard** provides an overview of your Atlaset activity and travel data. From the Dashboard, you can view your exploration progress, achievements, recent activity and statistics, as well as access your profile.

## Your personal overview

The dashboard provides a quick summary of your progress:

- **Countries visited**: Shows the number of countries you have visited compared with the total number of countries. Select the card to open your exploration progress.
- **Achievements**: Shows your completed achievements compared with the total number of available achievements. Select the card to view your achievements.
- **Statistics**: Opens the Statistics section, where you can view and analyze detailed information about your travel history.

## Recent activity

The **Recent Activity** section displays your five latest activity actions in Atlaset. By clicking **Activity**, you can navigate to the full activity log.
