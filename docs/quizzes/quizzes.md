# Quizzes

Atlaset features interactive geography `quizzes` to help you learn and test your knowledge about the world. Compete for high scores, challenge your `friends` and climb the `leaderboards`!

## **Quiz types**

- **`Guess the Flag`:** Guess `countries` based on the `flag`.
- **`Guess the Capital`:** Guess `capital cities` of `countries`.

## **Choosing difficulty**

`Quizzes` are divided into four `difficulty levels`:

- **Easy:** Familiar countries you likely know
- **Medium:** A mix of common and less-known countries
- **Hard:** Challenging countries that will test your knowledge
- **Expert:** Obscure countries and dependencies, only for true experts

> **Note:**
>
> Each difficulty level has its own countries!

## **Choosing game mode**

`Quizzes` currently support two `game modes`:

- **Sandbox:** Play freely without a timer or competition.
- **Timed:** Race against the clock to reach the highest score.

## **How to play**

1. **Start a quiz:**
   - Go to the `Quizzes` section from the `main menu` or `dashboard`.
   - Choose your `quiz type`, `difficulty` and `game mode`.
2. **Answer questions:**
   - Enter your answer in the provided form.
   - Get instant feedback and move to the next question.
3. **Game over:**
   - See your `score`, `correct answers` and `stats`.
   - Check the `leaderboards` for the best scores!

## **Leaderboards**

When playing in `timed mode`, your scores might enter the `leaderboards`! Only the top 25 highest entries are saved, so make sure you do your best!
