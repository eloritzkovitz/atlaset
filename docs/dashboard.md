# Dashboard

The **`Dashboard`** in Atlaset is your personal travel hub, providing a comprehensive overview of your journeys, achievements and travel statistics. It is designed to help you analyze your travel history, celebrate milestones, and discover new goals—all in one place.

## **Dashboard Features**

### **Countries**

The **`Countries`** section gives you a detailed view of your `visited` and `unvisited countries`, `regions`, and `subregions`. You can:

- **Browse and search** all `countries`, filter by `region` or `subregion` and quickly find places you've visited or want to visit.
- **View visited status**: Instantly see which `countries` you've visited, with visual highlights and `badges`.
- **Explore details**: Click on any `country` to view more information, including `flags`, `sovereignty` and `visit history`.
- **Toggle views**: Switch between `grid` and `list` layouts for your preferred browsing experience.

### **Achievements**

The **`Achievements`** section tracks your personal travel `milestones` and `goals`. Here you can:

- **Earn achievements** for visiting new `countries`, completing `collections` or reaching travel `milestones`.
- **View progress**: See which `achievements` you've unlocked, which are in progress and what you can aim for next.
- **Achievement details**: Each `achievement` includes a description, status and progress indicator.
- **Motivation**: Use `achievements` to set new travel `goals` and celebrate your journey.

### **Trips**

The **`Trips`** section provides insights and analytics about your travel history. Features include:

- **Trip overview**: See a summary of all your `trips`, including `total trips`, `countries visited` and `travel frequency`.
- **Yearly and monthly breakdowns**: Analyze your travel patterns over time, with charts and tables for each `year` and `month`.
- **Trip history**: Browse a `timeline` of your `trips`, with details on `destinations`, `dates` and `notes`.
- **Favorite trips**: Highlight and revisit your most memorable journeys.

> **Tips:**
> - **Keep your trip data up to date** to back up your travel history or share with friends.
> - **Check achievements regularly** to stay motivated and discover new travel goals.
