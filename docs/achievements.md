# Achievements

The **`Achievements`** section tracks your personal travel milestones and goals, based primarily on your trip and visit data but also with interaction with the application. For convenience, achievements are grouped into `types`, making them easier to follow.

You can search and filter achievements by name, type or status at the top of the achievements section.

## **Earning Achievements**

Achievements are earned automatically by achieving the required criteria. This can be visiting a certain count of countries, specific countries in a region, subregion or feature, visiting a country more than once and so on.

## **Tracking Progress**

Each `achievement` shows its completion status and progress towards completion. Some achievements can be tiered, and the full tier list can be seen by hovering over the progress indicator.
