# Saving Maps

Both shared and self-made maps can be saved for reference and future use. You can do so by clicking **`Save`** in the `map toolbar` when viewing the map.

Saved maps and their data are independent of the main user data and do not interfere with the user's `layers` and `markers`, making them flexible to use.

## **Managing Saved Maps**

Saved maps can be viewed in the `Saved` panel, accessible from the user menu or by pressing the <kbd>B</kbd> key. You can view, edit, rename or delete saved maps.

## **Edit Mode**

Saved maps can be viewed and edited in `Edit Mode`. When in `Edit Mode`, you can update a saved map's `layers` and `markers` through their respective panels in the `map toolbar` without having to recreate anything.
