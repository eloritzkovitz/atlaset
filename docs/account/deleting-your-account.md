# Deleting your account

You can delete your Atlaset account from the account settings.

> <icon name="warning"></icon> **Warning**
>
> **Deleting your account permanently removes your account and its associated data. This action cannot be reversed.**

## Back up your data

efore deleting your account, **it is recommended that you back up any data you want to keep**, as it will no longer be recoverable after your account has been deleted.

- [Exporting map data](/docs/atlas/layers-markers#importingexporting-map-data)
- [Exporting and sharing maps](docs/atlas/exporting-sharing-maps)
- [Exporting trips](/docs/trips/creating-managing-trips#importingexporting-trips)

## Hibernating your account

If you want to temporarily deactivate your account without deleting it, you can choose to **hibernate** your account.

Hibernating your account marks it as **deactivated** until you log in again. Your account and its associated data are preserved while it is hibernated.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, follow the next steps:

1. In the sidebar, click <icon name="settings"></icon> **Settings**.
2. Under **Account management**, click on <icon name="delete"></icon> **Delete account**.
3. Confirm the deletion in the confirmation dialog.

After you confirm, you will be logged out and returned to the homepage. Your account and associated data will then be permanently deleted.
