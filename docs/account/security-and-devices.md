# Security and devices

The **Security** section provides information about your account and allows you to manage the devices and sessions currently signed in to Atlaset.

## Security information

You can find a display of your security information:

|                   |                                                            |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Email             | The email address associated with your account.            |
| Account created   | The date your account was created.                         |
| Last login        | The most recent recorded login.                            |
| Last login method | The authentication method used for your most recent login. |

## Managing devices

The **Logged-in Devices** section shows all active sessions associated with your account. Each session represents a device or browser currently signed in to Atlaset.

Each session displays information about the browser and device, including the signed-in device, IP address, approximate location and whether the session is currently active or when it was last active.

You can also terminate individual sessions to sign out of Atlaset on a specific device.

### Terminating a session

1. In the sidebar, click <icon name="settings"></icon> **Settings**.
2. Under **Account management**, click on <icon name="delete"></icon> **Delete account**.
3. Under **Logged-in devices**, find the session and click <icon name="poweroff"></icon> **End this session**.
