# Progress

The **Progress** section provides an overview of your exploration progress across the world, organized by regions and subregions. It shows how many countries you've visited and lets you explore your progress at different geographic levels.

## World exploration

The **World Exploration** card displays your total number of visited countries compared with the total number of countries included in the current view. Your progress is also shown as a percentage and with a progress bar.

You can switch between displaying **all countries** and **sovereign only** to change which countries are included in the progress calculations.

## Regional progress

Countries are grouped by region, with each region displaying its visited and total country counts and overall progress.

You can sort regions **alphabetically** or by **progress percentage**, in ascending or descending order.

## Subregional progress

Each region can be explored further through its subregions. Subregions display their visited and total country counts, allowing you to see how your exploration is distributed within each region.

## Viewing countries

While **Progress** provides an overview of your exploration, the **Countries** view lets you view the relevant countries in detail.

By clicking on the **World Exploation** card, or any region or subregion, you will be able to explore a full country list associated with the selected view level.
