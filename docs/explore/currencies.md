# Currencies

The **Currencies** section provides a searchable list of currencies, including their names and **ISO 4217 codes**. You can search and filter currencies by name or code.

Open a currency to view its details, including the countries and territories that use it.

## Currency exchange

The **Currency Exchange** widget lets you convert an amount between two currencies.

- Select the currencies you want to convert between.
- Enter the amount to convert.
- Click **Convert** to calculate the exchange.

By default, the selected currencies are based on your home country's currency and USD.

Currency exchange rates are provided by the [Open Exchange Rates API](https://openexchangerates.org).
